Reports Overview covers jobs, clients, invoices, products, revenue, outstanding balances, job pipeline, product-location counts, document imports, top-selling items and supplier-invoice activity.

## Start with the question

- **Operational demand:** review jobs by type, month and status, plus pipeline views.
- **Revenue:** distinguish paid revenue from invoiced or pending amounts.
- **Collections:** review outstanding, overdue, Top Debtors and Debtor Exposure.
- **Inventory:** review product-location counts and top-selling items.
- **Supplier processing:** review imported documents by supplier and supplier invoices by month.

## Reconciliation method

1. Set and record the date range.
2. Write down the status included by the report.
3. Compare like-for-like entities and states.
4. Open the underlying list or records when available.
5. Explain any remaining difference before publishing or acting on the result.

<Warning>
  Controlled records produced different counts across some job lists, Reports Overview cards, invoice tabs and pipeline widgets. Pending invoices contributed to an outstanding balance but not paid revenue. Do not compare labels without confirming their definitions.
</Warning>
