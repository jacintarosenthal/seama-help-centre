Inventory records the items you buy, sell and consume. Stock locations, transfers and adjustments explain where quantities changed; Workorders can commit stock before invoice approval.

## Item types

- **Goods** can carry purchase cost, selling price, stock and reorder settings.
- **Service** items represent non-stock work such as labour services.
- **Composite items** package component items and can commit component stock when added to work.

<Warning>
  Do not reconcile stock from a single overview label. In testing, an overview value labelled **Stock on hand** matched available stock rather than the sum of location on-hand quantities.
</Warning>
