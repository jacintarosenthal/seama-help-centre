<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the transfer">
    Choose the source and destination locations, then add each item and quantity to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review available stock">
    Confirm the source can supply the requested quantity without consuming stock already committed to jobs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the transfer">
    Save the transfer and record its transaction number. If receipt confirmation is enabled, treat the stock as in transit until the destination accepts it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm receipt">
    Open the transfer at the destination and confirm the received quantities. Investigate differences rather than accepting an incorrect quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload and verify">
    Reload the transfer and item. Confirm the status and the destination location's on-hand quantity.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<StaleState />

<Note>
  The tested creation message said a transfer was “completed” even though it remained in transit and required receipt. Use the transaction status and destination quantity—not the success toast alone—to determine completion.
</Note>
