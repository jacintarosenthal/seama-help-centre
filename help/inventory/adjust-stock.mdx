<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the current location quantity">
    Open the item and note the on-hand, committed and available values for the location you will change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start an adjustment">
    Select the item and location, then enter a clear reason that explains the physical count or correction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the adjustment amount">
    Use a positive amount to add stock or a negative amount to remove stock. Calculate it from the difference between the recorded and verified physical quantities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and reload">
    Save the adjustment, record its transaction number, reload the item and verify the new location quantity and transaction history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  In controlled testing, **Actual counted quantity** did not persist during entry, while **Adjustment amount** did. Until retested, use the adjustment amount and verify the saved result after reload.
</Warning>
