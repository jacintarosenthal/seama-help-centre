## Before you begin

- Confirm the invoice recipient, line items, quantities, prices, tax, due date and total.
- Confirm the connected Xero organisation and intended publish status.
- Resolve any Seama prompt asking you to unlink and reconnect Xero before processing live records.
- Know whether the action will both approve and send the invoice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Open the record from **Pending Approval** and verify its current Seama and Xero statuses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the accounting detail">
    Compare the invoice with the completed job and confirm tax, due date, account-related settings and customer details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the consequence">
    Read the confirmation message for **Send to Xero**. In the tested workflow, the action stated that it would approve and sync the invoice.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit once">
    Confirm only when the integration is healthy. Wait for the result and reload the invoice before taking another action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify in both systems">
    Check the refreshed Xero status in Seama, then locate and validate the invoice directly in Xero. Confirm customer, lines, tax, total and Xero lifecycle state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If submission fails

The tested invoice returned **Error updating invoice bulk** and remained **Not synced**. Record the invoice ID and error, check integration health, and verify Xero for a partial or duplicate result before retrying.

<Warning>
  Direct Xero-side invoice verification was not completed in the 26 August 2026 audit because the browser session had expired. Treat this procedure as an operational safeguard, and obtain product-owner approval after completing the gated Xero test.
</Warning>
