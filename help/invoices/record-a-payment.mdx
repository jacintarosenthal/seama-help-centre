<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the correct invoice">
    Confirm the invoice number, client and total before entering payment information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the payment">
    Enter the received amount and choose the actual payment method, such as **Bank Transfer** or **Card**. Add the payment date and reference when available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and reconcile">
    Confirm **Paid** increased by the saved amount and **Outstanding** decreased by the same amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add any remaining payment separately">
    For split payments, create one entry per actual receipt and payment method. Do not replace history with a single combined entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check independent states">
    After the balance reaches zero, separately inspect invoice approval and Xero sync status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The payment history reflects the real receipts and the invoice balance is updated. A zero balance can coexist with **Pending Approval** or **Not synced**, so resolve those states through their own workflows.
