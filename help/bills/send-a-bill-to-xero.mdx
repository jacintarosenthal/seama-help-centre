## Before you begin

- The bill is **Ready** and still matches the source after reload.
- The supplier is linked or created correctly.
- The intended Xero account is selected and still present after reload.
- The Xero connection does not show a relink warning.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the individual bill">
    Use the bill record rather than assuming a batch action can complete missing supplier or account information. Controlled batch submission asked reviewers to open each bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm required details">
    Recheck supplier, account, tax, invoice number, dates, lines and total. If the account field is empty or has no options, stop and resolve integration health.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send once">
    Use the bill's Xero action and wait for completion. Reload before deciding whether it succeeded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the refreshed status">
    A successful controlled submission reloaded with Xero status **DRAFT** and an option to send again. Do not use that option without checking Xero first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify directly in Xero">
    Locate the bill and compare supplier, account, tax and totals with the source and Seama record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  The audit confirmed Seama-side **DRAFT** status for two bills, but direct Xero-side inspection remained gated by an expired Xero session. Complete that verification before treating this article as fully approved for live accounting use.
</Warning>
