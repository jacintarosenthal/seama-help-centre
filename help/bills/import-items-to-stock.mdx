<Steps>
  <Step title="Finish the bill review">
    Confirm the supplier, line identity, quantity, purchase cost, tax and total against the source document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the intended lines">
    Select only the rows that should become or update inventory items. Keep job-specific or non-stock costs out of the selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the destination">
    Choose **Assign to Stock Location** and select the exact receiving location for each line. Use the separate **Assign to Job Card** destination for job costs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before import">
    Recheck item codes, descriptions, quantities and location. Imported rows became locked in controlled testing, so correct destination mistakes now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and verify">
    Run the import, reload the destination items and confirm their location-level on-hand quantities and transaction history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The selected lines appear in inventory at the chosen location with the reviewed quantities. The bill's Xero status remains a separate workflow.
