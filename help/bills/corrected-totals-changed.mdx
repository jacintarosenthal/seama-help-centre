## Problem

The bill preview matches the source, but the saved or reloaded total is different.

## Likely cause

The tested receipt correction applied a GST adjustment again after server normalisation. A preview showed **$29.64**, but reload showed **$3.33** until the adjustment was reset to zero.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Stop downstream actions">
    Do not mark the bill **Ready**, import lines or send it to Xero while totals disagree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reopen the source comparison">
    Record the authoritative net, tax and total from the source image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect tax and adjustment fields">
    Look for a correction that duplicates a value Seama has already normalised. Reset or recalculate the adjustment only when the line and tax evidence supports it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, reload and compare">
    Save once, reload the bill, then compare net, tax and final total with the source again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate if it repeats">
    Keep the bill in **To Review**. Provide the bill ID, source total, expected tax, before-and-after values, timestamp and redacted screenshots to support.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Never “balance” the bill by changing a source value without evidence. The source document remains authoritative.
</Warning>
