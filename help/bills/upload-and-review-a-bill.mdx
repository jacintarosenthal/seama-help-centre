## Before you begin

- Use a clear source document and know the authoritative total.
- Confirm the remaining monthly upload allowance. Email ingestion also consumed an allowance in testing.
- Have the intended supplier, account, tax treatment and line destinations available.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the document">
    Upload the file or email it to the tenant-specific inbound invoice address. Wait for processing and parsing to finish before editing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the source and extracted data together">
    Keep the document image visible while reviewing the extracted fields. Do not review from the Seama totals alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate document identity">
    Compare supplier, ABN, invoice number, invoice date, due date, order references and address with the source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate every line">
    Compare product codes, descriptions, quantities, unit costs, tax and line totals. Correct fabricated, blank or corrupted values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate accounting totals">
    Compare net, GST or tax, adjustments and final total with the source. Choose the intended Xero account and add an internal note for material corrections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose line destinations">
    Decide whether lines belong in inventory, on a job card, or are split. Make this decision before import locks processed rows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save, reload and compare again">
    Save the changes, reload the bill and repeat the source-total comparison. Do not rely on the pre-save preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the bill Ready">
    Change **To Review** to **Ready** only when the reloaded record matches the source and all required supplier, account and destination decisions are complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A photographed receipt in testing misread GST, total, date, address, ABN, product codes and descriptions. Another correction previewed the right total but reloaded incorrectly because a GST adjustment was applied again. Always perform the reload check.
</Warning>

## Emailed documents and duplicates

An emailed attachment successfully created a **To Review** record and used one upload allowance. Duplicate behavior was inconsistent for a repeated invoice number, so inspect supplier, number, date and total before accepting or resending a document.
