Create the job record first, then add a booking for when the work will occur.

## Before you begin

- Confirm the client, site and contact details.
- Select a geocoded address suggestion if the job must appear correctly on maps.
- Decide whether the job starts as a **Quote** or **Workorder**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the job">
    Open the jobs area and start a new job. Select the client and site, then enter the job title or description and required dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the starting status and type">
    Select **Quote** when approval is needed before work. Select **Workorder** for active work. Confirm the job type and queue if your organisation uses them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add operational context">
    Add the site contact, job contacts, tasks or to-dos, a reminder and any internal notes needed by the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a booking">
    Add the booking date, start time and duration. Select the staff member or members who should attend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and verify">
    Confirm the booking card shows the expected staff and time. Separately check the job header's **Assigned To** value and correct it if your workflow requires a job-level assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The job is available in the relevant job list and has a booking for the planned work. Filter settings can hide it from some lists, so search by job number before recreating a missing record.

## Troubleshooting

- If the map cannot locate the job, reselect the address from the location suggestions rather than saving typed text alone.
- If the booking has staff but **Assigned To** says **Not assigned**, see [booking assignment versus job assignment](/help/jobs/booking-vs-job-assignment).
