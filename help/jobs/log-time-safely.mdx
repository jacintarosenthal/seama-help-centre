<Warning>
  The tested time-entry dialog retained more than one selected staff member and a previous time type. Recheck both **Staff** and **Type** immediately before saving every entry.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open time entry from the job">
    Start a time entry on the correct job and confirm the work date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose staff deliberately">
    Remove any staff who should not receive the entry, then select only the intended person or people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the time type">
    Select **Job** for work time or **Travel** for travel time. Do not rely on the previously displayed value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter duration and save">
    Add the start and finish or duration required by the form. Review staff, type and duration one last time, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the result">
    Check the job's total hours and the staff timesheet. If labour cost matters, confirm the entry used the expected staff labour rate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The job separates job time and travel time, and the staff timesheet contains the entry for the intended person and date.
