## Job lifecycle

Use **Quote** for proposed work and **Workorder** for active work. A Quote can be converted to a Workorder; completed operational work can then move into invoicing. **Cancelled** represents work that will not proceed.

## Related job tools

- **Tasks and to-dos** track work that must be completed.
- **Reminders** support date-based follow-up in calendar and agenda views.
- **Notes** preserve job context and appear in recent activity.
- **Bookings** reserve time and staff.
- **Queues** group work for operational handling.
- **Job templates** speed up repeat creation, but template metadata and inherited job fields should be reviewed after creation.
