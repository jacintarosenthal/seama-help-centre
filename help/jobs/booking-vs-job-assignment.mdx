A booking answers “who is scheduled at this date and time?” The job header's **Assigned To** field represents job-level assignment. In the tested product, adding staff to a booking did not populate **Assigned To**.

## What to check

1. Open the booking and confirm its staff, date and time.
2. Open the job header and check **Assigned To** separately.
3. Set the job-level assignment if your **My Jobs**, reporting or operational workflow depends on it.
4. Reload the job and confirm both values.

<Warning>
  Do not remove or recreate a valid booking just because **Assigned To** is empty. First identify which assignment your team actually needs to change.
</Warning>
