<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the Quote">
    Create a job with the **Quote** status. Confirm the client, site, contact and description of the proposed work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add quoted items">
    Add products, services or composite items with the intended quantities, selling prices, tax and explanatory text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the customer document">
    Generate the Quote PDF or preview and check business details, recipient, scope, prices, tax, terms and final total before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and record the decision">
    Send the Quote through the approved communication path. Record the client's acceptance or decline using the current Seama controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Convert accepted work">
    Convert the accepted Quote to a **Workorder**. Recheck status, job type, assignment, booking, tasks and item details before scheduling work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The active job is a Workorder and retains the customer and proposed-work context needed for delivery.

<Note>
  Seama uses the interface label **Workorder**. Search metadata also includes “work order” so users can find the same guidance with either spelling.
</Note>
