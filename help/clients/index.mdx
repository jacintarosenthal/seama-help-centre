Keep the client, work site and people as distinct records. Jobs can then use the correct service address and contact while invoices use the intended billing details and payment terms.

## Data-quality rules

- Search before adding a client to reduce duplicates.
- Keep client contacts and site contacts in the right record.
- Confirm contact roles and the intended recipient before sending a message.
- Select a geocoded address suggestion when dispatch maps are required.
- Review client job and invoice history before changing financial details.
