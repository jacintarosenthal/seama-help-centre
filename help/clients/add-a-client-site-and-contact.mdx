<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for the client">
    Search by name, email and other known identifiers. Update an existing record when it represents the same customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the client">
    Add the client identity, general and billing details, email address and payment terms. Save and confirm the record is searchable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a named site">
    Add the service-site name and address. Choose an address suggestion rather than typing plain text when map coordinates matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add client and site contacts">
    Store the people responsible for client-wide, billing or site-specific communication in the appropriate record. Add their contact roles where available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify on a test job">
    Create or open a test job and confirm the client details, site address and contacts appear independently and correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The job can use the correct client, job address and contact, while billing retains its own recipient and payment terms.

<Note>
  A saved text address can still display **Map coordinates not available**. If dispatch mapping is required, edit the site and select a geocoded suggestion.
</Note>
