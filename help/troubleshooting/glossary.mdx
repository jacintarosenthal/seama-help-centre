## Jobs

**Quote**  
Proposed work that can be sent for a customer decision and converted to a Workorder.

**Workorder**  
The exact Seama label for active work. Also commonly searched as “work order.”

**Completed**  
A job state indicating operational work has been marked complete. It does not itself prove invoice approval, payment or Xero sync.

**Cancelled**  
A job state for work that will not proceed.

**Booking**  
A scheduled period with date, time, duration and staff. Booking staff can differ from the job's **Assigned To** value.

**Queue**  
A configured grouping used to organise jobs for operational handling.

**Job type**  
A configured classification used in job creation, filters and reporting.

## Invoices and Xero

**Pending Approval**  
An invoice workflow state indicating approval has not been completed. A paid invoice may still appear here.

**Pending Payment**  
An invoice collection state or view for balances awaiting payment. Confirm the exact inclusion rules in the current product.

**Not synced**  
Seama does not show a completed Xero submission for the record. Search Xero before retrying if a previous attempt may have partially succeeded.

**DRAFT**  
A Xero status returned to Seama for tested supplier bills. Verify the actual record directly in Xero.

## Inventory

**On hand**  
Physical quantity recorded at a stock location.

**Committed**  
Quantity reserved by active work, including items added to a Workorder.

**Available**  
Quantity not committed. In the tested records, it matched on hand minus committed.

**Composite item**  
A packaged item that expands to component items and quantities when used.

## Bills and documents

**Inbox**  
The entry area for supplier documents received by supported paths.

**To Review**  
A document awaiting human validation and required decisions.

**Ready**  
A document that a reviewer has prepared for the next workflow step. It does not itself mean the bill is synced to Xero.

**Archive**  
The area for documents no longer active in the main processing views.

**OCR**  
Automated extraction of text and values from a document image. It can be wrong and must be checked against the source.
