## A job is missing from My Jobs

1. Search by job number in **All Jobs**.
2. Clear date, job type, status, staff and queue filters.
3. Open the job and inspect **Assigned To**.
4. Check whether the user only appears on a booking.
5. Review the user's job permissions.

## Booking staff and Assigned To disagree

A booking's staff and the job-level **Assigned To** field behaved independently in testing. Review and correct the field your workflow relies on. Do not delete a valid booking solely because the header says **Not assigned**.

## An address has no map coordinates

1. Edit the client or site address.
2. Start typing the address and select the correct geocoded suggestion.
3. Save and reload the record.
4. Reopen the job or map view.

A typed address can save as text without coordinates.

## Time was saved to the wrong staff member or type

Open the time entry and check all selected staff plus **Job** versus **Travel**. The dialog retained previous selections in testing. Correct or remove the entry according to permissions, then verify job totals and the staff timesheet.

## On hand, committed and available disagree

Reconcile location-level on hand, active job commitment, received transfers, adjustments and bill imports. The item overview's **Stock on hand** label matched available stock in the tested scenario, so prefer location detail for the audit.

## A transfer still shows in transit

Check whether receipt confirmation is required. Open the transfer, confirm destination receipt, reload, and verify the destination quantity. Ignore a generic “completed” toast when the transaction status still requires receipt.

## A stock adjustment did not apply

Use **Adjustment amount** rather than relying on **Actual counted quantity**, which did not persist in testing. Save, reload, and confirm the location quantity and transaction record.

## A corrected bill total changed after reload

Keep the bill in **To Review** and stop inventory or Xero actions. Compare the source net, tax and total, inspect adjustments that may have been applied twice, save once, reload and compare again. See [the full recovery procedure](/help/bills/corrected-totals-changed).

## An invoice or bill is Not synced

1. Reload and record the current Seama and Xero statuses.
2. Confirm the connected Xero organisation and whether Seama requests a relink.
3. Check required client or supplier, account, tax, number, date and total details.
4. Search directly in Xero for the record before retrying.
5. If the account field disappeared or has no options, stop and restore integration health.
6. Retry only after you know no duplicate exists.

## Report totals differ

Match date range, entity, status and revenue basis. Pending invoices contributed to outstanding balance but not paid revenue in testing. Document the definitions before reconciling widgets or exports.

## If the issue continues

Provide support with the record ID, user role, current and expected state, timestamp, reproduction steps, visible error text and a cropped screenshot with sensitive data removed.
