<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the role first">
    Review the duties the person performs and select an existing default or custom role with the least required access. Create and test a custom role before using it widely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the staff identity">
    Create the staff record with the person's name, unique work email, job title and required contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add operational details">
    Enter the labour rate used for job costing and the emergency contact details required by your organisation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the security role">
    Select the approved role and save the staff record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test access">
    Have the user sign in safely and confirm they can perform expected tasks without seeing unrelated administration or financial areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add leave if needed">
    Add current or planned leave and confirm it appears in the staff record and relevant scheduling context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The staff member can access the parts of Seama needed for their role, and time entries can use the configured labour rate for cost reporting.
