## Role types

- **Default roles** provide a predefined permission set and were read-only in controlled testing.
- **Custom roles** can be edited to match an organisation's responsibilities.

## Permission areas to review

- Dashboard and operational views
- Jobs and job cards
- Clients and invoices
- Items, composites and inventory movements
- Documents and bills
- Staff, roles and organisation settings

## Create or change a custom role

1. Name the role for the responsibility, not a specific person.
2. Start with only the permissions needed for the role's core tasks.
3. Review create, edit, approve, delete, export and financial consequences separately.
4. Save and assign the role to a synthetic test user.
5. Sign in as that user and test expected and restricted actions.
6. Obtain owner approval before assigning the role broadly.

<Tip>
  When a page or action is missing, compare the user's role with a working user's role before assuming the feature is unavailable.
</Tip>
