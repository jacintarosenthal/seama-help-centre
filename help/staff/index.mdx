## Key rules

- Default roles are read-only.
- Custom roles are editable and can contain a broad permission set across dashboards, jobs, clients, invoices, items, documents, staff, roles and settings.
- Assign the least access needed for the person's duties.
- A missing page or action may be a permission outcome rather than a product error.
- Deactivate access when a person no longer needs Seama; preserve operational records according to policy.
