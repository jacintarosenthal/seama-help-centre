## Tested Xero setup state

- Xero was connected and enabled.
- The organisation was configured to publish documents as **Draft**.
- Integration settings later displayed **Please unlink your Xero account and link it again** instead of a selectable default payment account.
- Unlinking was not performed because it is consequential and requires fresh authentication.
