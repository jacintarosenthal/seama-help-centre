## Track each dimension separately

| Dimension | Question to answer | Where to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seama content | Are identity, lines, tax, dates and totals correct? | Open the Seama record and source/job |
| Seama workflow | Is the record To Review, Ready, Pending Approval or another state? | Seama status and queue |
| Seama sync | Is it Not synced, DRAFT or another returned status? | Seama Xero status after reload |
| Xero lifecycle | Does the record exist and is it Draft, approved, paid, voided or deleted? | Directly in the connected Xero organisation |
| Payment | What was received and what remains outstanding? | Payment history and balance; reconcile with accounting policy |

## What the audit established

- Two individual supplier bills reloaded in Seama with Xero status **DRAFT**.
- A batch of five bills did not submit because records needed supplier and account details.
- Some bill account selections disappeared or returned with no options.
- One invoice submission failed with **Error updating invoice bulk** and stayed **Not synced**.
- One invoice became fully paid in Seama but remained **Pending Approval** and **Not synced**.

## Still gated

Direct Xero-side inspection of the submitted bills, invoice approval, payment reconciliation, and correction or voiding flows was not completed because the Xero browser session expired. Xero reconnect behavior also remains a controlled administrator decision.

<Warning>
  Never retry based only on an old Seama screen. Reload, search Xero for the record, and check integration health first.
</Warning>
