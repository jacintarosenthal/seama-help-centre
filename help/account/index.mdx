## Profile and company

- Edit your profile details, home location and profile photo.
- Maintain business identity and ABN, general and billing emails, office location and company logo.
- Change your password and enable email two-step verification from the appropriate security area.

## Plans and billing

Review the current plan, job-card limits, import limits and SMS limits in the live Billing area before making operational commitments. Use the Stripe billing portal only through Seama's approved billing action to update payment details or download subscription invoices.

<Warning>
  Plan names, allowances and billing inclusions can change. Use the values displayed in the signed-in account rather than copying exact limits from an older screenshot or article.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The audited Billing area contained placeholder copy and duplicated inclusion concepts. Treat the account's actual usage counters, invoice history and payment portal as stronger evidence than descriptive placeholder text.
</Note>
