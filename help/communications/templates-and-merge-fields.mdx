<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the template">
    Choose email or SMS, give the template a clear task-based name, and write content that remains understandable if an optional field is missing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert fields from the supported list">
    Use the merge-field picker rather than guessing placeholder names. Controlled tests confirmed `{client_name}`, `{company_name}` for email, and `{job_number}`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview from a synthetic job">
    Open the template from a controlled job and check every placeholder in the draft. A raw token means the field did not resolve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check email subject and body">
    Review both fields after reopening the editor. In testing, the list showed a saved subject while the editor loaded its subject box blank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to an authorised destination">
    Send the email or SMS only to a controlled recipient. Confirm email delivery or opened activity and the SMS acceptance result.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Do not use `{job_no}`. It remained literal in both tested email and SMS drafts. Use `{job_number}` from the supported merge-field list.
</Warning>
