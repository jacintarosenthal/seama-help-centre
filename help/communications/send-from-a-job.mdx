<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm the recipient">
    Review the job's client, billing and site contacts. Choose the person responsible for this message and verify the email address or mobile number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the message from the job">
    Choose email or SMS so the communication remains in the job's recent activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply a template if useful">
    Select the approved template and inspect the merged subject and body. Replace any unresolved token before sending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review attachments and content">
    Confirm attachments belong to this customer and contain no unrelated information. For SMS, keep the message within the displayed limit and allowance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send once and verify">
    Submit the message and check the job activity for the accepted, sent or opened state available for the channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Result

The communication is addressed to the intended contact, contains resolved job information and appears in recent job activity.
