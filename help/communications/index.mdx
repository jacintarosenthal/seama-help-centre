## Tested behavior

- Email and SMS templates resolved `{client_name}`.
- Email resolved `{company_name}`.
- `{job_no}` remained literal and should not be used.
- `{job_number}` resolved to the current job number in both email and SMS drafts.
- Sent email activity changed to opened after the controlled recipient opened it.
- The controlled SMS was accepted and reduced the allowance.

<Note>
  SMS content and recipient numbers should be reviewed before sending. The current plan records a 150-character content limit and monthly allowance as important customer guidance; recheck the live product before publishing exact plan limits.
</Note>
