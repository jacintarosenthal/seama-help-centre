Seama connects customer records, jobs, field activity, stock, supplier bills, invoices and accounting hand-off. Use this guide to learn the workflow in a safe order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the workflow">
    Learn how Quote, Workorder, Completed, invoice, payment and Xero states relate—and where they remain independent.

    <Card title="See the workflow" href="/getting-started/workflow" icon="workflow" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your organisation">
    Configure company details, team access, operational defaults, stock locations and Xero boundaries.

    <Card title="Set up Seama" href="/getting-started/setup" icon="settings" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete a safe first workflow">
    Use synthetic data to create a client, schedule work, log time and items, invoice, take payment and process a supplier bill.

    <Card title="Run the first workflow" href="/getting-started/first-workflow" icon="play" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare to go live">
    Confirm access, data, finance, inventory, training and test-data cleanup.

    <Card title="Use the go-live checklist" href="/getting-started/go-live" icon="list-checks" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep Getting Started short. Use the task pages linked from each step when you need detailed procedures.
</Tip>
