Use one clearly labelled test record set and follow it from creation to verification.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the customer context">
    Add a client, a named site and the relevant client and site contacts. Select a geocoded address suggestion when maps or dispatch coordinates matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and schedule work">
    Create a Quote or Workorder, add a booking, choose staff, and add a task, reminder and note. Check booking assignment and the job's **Assigned To** field separately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add activity and costs">
    Log job and travel time, rechecking both **Staff** and **Type** before saving. Add a goods item, service item or composite item and confirm expected stock commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and invoice">
    Mark the Workorder complete, create the invoice, then check descriptions, quantities, tax, due date and total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test approval and payment states">
    If Xero is healthy and the test is authorised, approve and send the invoice, then verify it in Xero. Record a controlled payment and confirm **Paid**, **Outstanding**, approval and Xero status independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process a supplier bill">
    Upload a synthetic bill, compare all extracted values with the source, choose destinations, save, reload, recheck totals, import controlled lines, and mark the bill **Ready**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Expected result

You can trace the same synthetic scenario across customer, job, booking, time, stock, invoice, payment and bill records. Any mismatch is logged and resolved—or consciously accepted—before live use.
