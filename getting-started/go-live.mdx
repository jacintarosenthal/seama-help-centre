## Company and configuration

- [ ] Business identity, ABN, emails, office location and logo are correct.
- [ ] Default job status, job types, queues and payment terms match the operating process.
- [ ] Markup bands, account codes and tax rates have finance approval.
- [ ] Stock locations and document templates are ready.

## Staff and security

- [ ] Every active staff member can sign in.
- [ ] Labour rates and emergency contacts are complete where required.
- [ ] Default or custom roles grant only the permissions each person needs.
- [ ] Two-step verification and account-recovery responsibilities are understood.

## Data

- [ ] Client, supplier and inventory imports were reviewed for duplicates.
- [ ] Site addresses that need maps were selected from geocoded suggestions.
- [ ] A pre-migration export is retained.
- [ ] Synthetic test records are clearly labelled for retention or scheduled for safe removal.

## Jobs, invoices and inventory

- [ ] The team completed a Quote-to-Workorder-to-Completed test.
- [ ] Booking assignment and job assignment are understood as separate fields.
- [ ] Time-entry users know to recheck **Staff** and **Type** before saving.
- [ ] On hand, committed and available stock values were reconciled.
- [ ] A transfer was received and an adjustment was verified after reload.
- [ ] Invoice approval, payment and Xero states were checked independently.

## Bills and Xero

- [ ] Bill reviewers compare supplier, number, dates, every line, tax and total with the source.
- [ ] Reviewers save, reload and compare totals again before **Ready**.
- [ ] Bill-line destinations are chosen before import locks rows.
- [ ] The correct Xero organisation, default account and publish status are confirmed.
- [ ] A controlled invoice and bill were verified in Xero before any retry.
- [ ] The team knows what to do if Seama asks to unlink and reconnect Xero.

## Team readiness

- [ ] Administrators, dispatchers, field staff and accounts staff know their first-day tasks.
- [ ] Help Centre links are shared in onboarding materials.
- [ ] A product owner and documentation owner are assigned.
- [ ] Support escalation includes record ID, visible status, expected result, timestamp and screenshot with sensitive data removed.

<Tip>
  Go live when every applicable check is complete and the accountable owner has accepted any documented exception.
</Tip>
