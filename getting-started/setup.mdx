Complete setup in this order so later records inherit the right defaults.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete profile and company details">
    Add business identity and contact information, office location, billing email and company logo. Set each user's home location if it will be used for dispatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Secure administrator access">
    Change temporary passwords and enable email two-step verification. Confirm which email address administrators can manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add staff and roles">
    Add each staff member, labour rate and emergency contact. Assign the least-privileged role that supports their work. Default roles are read-only; create a custom role when permissions need to change.

    [Add staff and assign a role →](/help/staff/add-staff-and-assign-a-role)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure operations">
    Choose the default job status, job types, queues, payment terms, markup bands, account and tax settings, stock locations, and job, email, SMS and document templates.

    [Configure operations →](/help/settings/configure-operations)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare data imports">
    Clean and deduplicate clients, items and suppliers before import. Export existing data before a material migration change and review import results before users start work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Xero carefully">
    Confirm the intended organisation, default account and publish status. Use controlled records for the first test and verify the result in Xero before processing live transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check your progress

- Staff can sign in and see only the areas they need.
- New jobs start in the intended default status.
- Stock locations, payment terms, markup, accounts and tax rates are available.
- Templates use tested merge fields and current business details.
- Imported data is free of obvious duplicates.
- The team knows that Seama and Xero states must be verified separately.
